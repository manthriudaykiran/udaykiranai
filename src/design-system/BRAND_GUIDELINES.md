> **Public Brand:** Uday Kiran | Practical AI

> **Visual Spirit:** Premium · Cinematic · Vibrant · Elegant · Futuristic · Powerful · Creative · Modern · Memorable  
> **Watermark Rule:** "Created by Uday Kiran" (Subtle, unobtrusive)

---

## 1. Executive Brand Identity

Uday Kiran's brand design system bridges two powerful aesthetic worlds:
1. **Warm Human Vitality:** Radiant Amber, Sunset Orange, Golden Glow, and Coral — representing optimism, warmth, clarity, and making AI accessible to all humans.
2. **Technological Sophistication:** Electric Magenta, Royal Violet, Electric Indigo, and Aqua Cyan — representing high-velocity cognitive architecture, autonomous AI systems, and mathematical elegance.

The resulting aesthetic combines **Apple-level visual restraint**, **Adobe-level creative color expression**, **high-end cinematic lighting**, and **modern SaaS precision**.

## 5. Color Proportion & Hierarchy Rule

- **60% Primary Neutral Canvas:** Midnight Navy (`#071426`) or Soft White (`#F6F9FC`).
- **25% Dominant Brand Gradient/Surface:** Hero title gradient, card boundaries, active containers.
- **10% Supporting Brand Color:** Secondary labels, icons, progress indicators.
- **5% Focal Accents:** Rare high-impact primary buttons, verified status marks.
- **Maximum 3 chromatic colors** per single composition.

---

## 6. Typography System

- **Display & Headlines:** `Sora` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (ExtraBold 800 or Bold 700) with `-0.025em` tracking.
- **Body Prose:** `Manrope` or `Inter` (Regular 400 or Medium 500) with `1.6` line-height.
- **Technical & Tabular:** `JetBrains Mono` (`font-mono-tech tabular-nums`) for latency, metrics, rates.
- **Telugu Content:** `Noto Sans Telugu` for authentic regional language typography.

---

## 7. Presentation & Social Templates

- **16:9 Decks:** One key idea per slide. Click-to-reveal bullet cadence. Watermark: `Created by Uday Kiran`.
- **Instagram Reels/Stories (9:16):** 1080×1920 with 250px vertical safe margins.
- **Instagram Feed (4:5):** 1080×1350 with high-contrast text over glassmorphic card.
- **YouTube Thumbnails (16:9):** 1280×720 with tested readability at 120px mobile width. Max 4-5 words.

---

## 8. Anti-AI Slop Enforcement

- **No Static Pill Enclosures:** Metadata is rendered unboxed with `·` separators.
- **No Mechanical Prefixes:** Avoid `// 01` or `>_` code comment labels.
- **No Decorative Pulsing Dots:** Green pulsing dots only allowed for real live streaming feeds.
- **Top Bar Contract:** Exactly 3 zones (1-line Brand wordmark, 4-5 nav links, 1 primary action button).
